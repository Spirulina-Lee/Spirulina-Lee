Hi 👋 I study electrical engineering. I did my undergrad at CQUPT and Northern Arizona University, and I'm now a grad student at the University of Washington.

Most of what's here is coursework and side projects, from microcontrollers and digital logic to web dashboards and LLM agents.

### Things I've made

- **[BMS-Alarm-Capstone](https://github.com/Spirulina-Lee/BMS-Alarm-Capstone)**: my senior capstone, a dashboard that filters out nuisance alarms in a campus building management system.
- **[llm-agents-ee500d](https://github.com/Spirulina-Lee/llm-agents-ee500d)**: an LLM agent that checks the weather and finds nearby places to visit.
- **[MathModeling](https://github.com/Spirulina-Lee/MathModeling)**: two modeling contest entries, one shaping an apple tree's canopy to catch more sunlight and one deciding when a factory should inspect its parts. Both won prizes.
- **[CompEng-Basics](https://github.com/Spirulina-Lee/CompEng-Basics)** and **[MSP430 labs](https://github.com/Spirulina-Lee/Microprocessor-Labs-MSP430-EE215)**: digital logic in HDL and low-level code on a microcontroller.
